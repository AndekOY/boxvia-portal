Privacy Policy

How BoxVia handles personal information when you visit the website, contact us, request a service or make a payment.

Last updated: 8 November 2026.

1. Who is responsible

Andrey Ekhmenin, trading as BoxVia, is a sole proprietor registered in Poland (NIP: 8513329301).

For personal data used to operate the website, respond to inquiries, manage service requests and administer customer relationships, BoxVia acts as a data controller.

Privacy contact: andrzej.ekhmenin@gmail.com.

For certain client-provided materials, BoxVia may instead act as a processor on the client's instructions; the roles and required contractual terms must be established before that processing.

2. Information we receive

When you contact us, we may receive your email address, message, correspondence and any details you choose to provide.

For Check a Claim, the form requests an email address, the exact claim, a public primary-source URL, an optional source version or date, and required confirmations.

For other service requests, the form requests your email address and service-specific descriptions, such as a question, system version, proposed scenario, expected behavior or available materials.

When a payment is made, we process transaction references and payment status associated with the order; payment details are handled by PayPal.

Technical and anti-abuse information may be processed when requests reach the website or when Cloudflare Turnstile checks a form submission.

The website also includes Cloudflare Web Analytics, which provides aggregate information about website usage. Its precise technical processing and any applicable consent requirements should be reviewed against the deployed configuration.

Please do not submit passwords, confidential documents, private access links, internal logs, special-category personal data or other sensitive information through public forms.

3. Why we process data and our legal bases

We use inquiry and order information to respond to requests, assess feasibility, take requested steps before entering a contract, perform agreed services and deliver results (GDPR Article 6(1)(b), where applicable).

For business representatives, routine correspondence and service administration may rely on our legitimate interests in managing professional relationships (Article 6(1)(f)), subject to applicable balancing and objection rights.

Billing and accounting records are processed where necessary to comply with legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).

Proportionate technical security and abuse-prevention measures may rely on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)).

These grounds do not authorize processing restricted client evidence for unrelated purposes.

4. How the website processes requests

Check a Claim submissions are processed by a Cloudflare Worker and recorded in a Cloudflare D1 database, together with information needed to track the request and its payment status.

Other service-request forms are processed by a Cloudflare Worker and sent to BoxVia as an administrative email through Resend.

Cloudflare Turnstile is used to check certain form submissions for abuse; its operation may involve technical information associated with the request.

PayPal processes checkout and payment information under its own privacy terms. BoxVia does not collect payment-card details through its own request form.

After payment confirmation, order-related administrative notifications may be sent through Resend. Service reports and correspondence are delivered using the agreed communication channel, normally email.

External services may process data under their own terms and in their applicable locations; this notice does not claim that every processing operation remains exclusively within the European Union.

5. Recipients and international transfers

Depending on the activity, relevant recipients or service providers include Cloudflare (website infrastructure, database, Web Analytics and anti-abuse checks), Resend (service-request notifications and related email delivery), PayPal (payments), and the providers of our email correspondence.

We share information only as needed for the described purposes or as legally required.

Some providers or their subprocessors may process information outside the EEA. Any transfer requiring safeguards under GDPR Chapter V must have an applicable legal transfer mechanism.

The precise provider configuration, locations and transfer mechanisms remain subject to operational verification; no guarantee of EU-only processing is made.

6. Retention and deletion

Inquiry and order information is retained only for as long as needed for its relevant purpose, including service administration, accounting obligations, dispute handling and applicable legal requirements.

Different categories may have different retention requirements, and provider backups or operational records may follow separate deletion schedules.

Specific retention periods and verified deletion behavior for the current infrastructure have not yet been finalized. We do not promise immediate or automatic deletion of every copy.

You may contact us to ask about retention or request deletion where the GDPR gives you that right.

7. Confidential materials and independent assessments

Public inquiry forms are intended for non-sensitive summaries and public links only.

Before receiving restricted evidence, confidential records, personal data on behalf of a client or system access, we must separately agree on scope, permissions, authorized recipients, transfer method, relevant service providers and any required confidentiality and data-processing agreements.

An NDA by itself does not authorize external sharing, AI-tool processing, production access or publication.

Client evidence, identifying client information and assessment reports are not made public merely because an engagement takes place. Proposed disclosures require a separate rights, confidentiality and recipient review.

Our internal BoxVia Confidential Assessment Framework (BCAF) documents are under development; their existence does not certify an environment or authorize restricted-data intake.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable conditions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability or object to certain processing.

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

You may complain to the competent data-protection authority, including the Polish Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

Send privacy requests to andrzej.ekhmenin@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

The website includes Cloudflare Web Analytics and Cloudflare Turnstile. Cloudflare and other providers may use technical storage or similar mechanisms needed for security and service operation. Actual cookies, storage, analytics configuration and any consent requirements must be checked against the deployed configuration.

Links to external websites, including PayPal and LinkedIn, lead to services governed by their own privacy notices.

This notice does not assert that the website is entirely cookie-free or that all tracking technologies have been exhaustively audited.
