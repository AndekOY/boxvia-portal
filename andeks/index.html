ANDEKS™ Normative System

ANDEKS™ provides a method and rules for independent assessment. It helps reviewers explain what the evidence supports about an AI system, a digital product or an organizational process.

Read release v0.3 on Zenodo

How an assessment works

  1. Define what is being assessed and under which conditions.
  2. Check what the evidence supports and record what remains unestablished.
  3. Explain the conclusion so another reviewer can trace it back to the evidence.

The assessment informs a decision. The decision remains with the person or organization responsible for it.

See the method in practice

Check a Claim is a small BoxVia demonstration of selected ANDEKS™ v0.3 principles. It checks one claim against one public primary source. A formal ANDEKS™ assessment follows the full applicable methodology.
