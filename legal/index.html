Service boundaries and data handling

BoxVia offers bounded analytical services. Each result is limited to its defined question, identified evidence and assessment scope.

Service boundaries

  • No certification, legal opinion, security audit, comprehensive audit or guaranteed regulatory outcome is provided unless separately and expressly agreed.
  • ANDEKS™ normative assessments are distinct from limited BoxVia commercial evidence checks. ARE and ERA research do not imply production execution or independent verification.
  • General service engagements require agreement on scope, inputs, deliverable and price before work begins.

Check a Claim: request and payment

The Check a Claim form accepts one precise claim, a public primary-source URL and an email address. The stated price is $20 USD. After form submission, the confirmation page provides an external PayPal checkout link. Submission does not constitute payment; review begins only after payment is verified. A report is delivered by email, normally as an approximately two-page English PDF.

If a request cannot be fulfilled within the defined scope, BoxVia will contact the requester to discuss a narrower scope or a refund, as applicable. The assessment may conclude Supported, Not supported or Unestablished within its stated boundary.

Data submitted through the form

For Check a Claim, the submitted email address, exact claim, source URL, optional version/date and required confirmations are received through Netlify Forms. This information is used to review the request, correspond with the requester, match the payment manually and deliver the result. Please submit only public, non-sensitive material. Do not provide passwords, confidential records or sensitive personal data.

Form processing and notifications use Netlify infrastructure; payments are handled separately on PayPal's website under PayPal's own terms and privacy notices. Payment card details are not collected by this BoxVia form. For questions about submitted data, contact andrzej.ekhmenin@gmail.com.
