Check a Claim: request and payment

The Check a Claim form accepts one precise claim, a public primary-source URL and an email address.

The stated price is $20 USD.

After form submission, the confirmation page opens a secure PayPal checkout for the corresponding request.

Submission does not constitute payment; review begins only after server-side payment confirmation.

The structured English-language PDF report, normally around two pages, is delivered to the customer's submitted email address within 24 hours after payment confirmation.

If a request cannot be fulfilled within the defined scope, BoxVia will contact the requester to discuss a narrower scope or a refund, as applicable.

The assessment may conclude Supported, Not supported or Unestablished within its stated boundary.

This information is used to review the request, correspond with the requester, associate the PayPal payment with the request and deliver the result.

Review engagements

Confidentiality

Start with a non-sensitive summary.

Before sharing confidential materials, agree in writing on what may be shared, how to transfer it and any confidentiality terms or NDA.

Use redacted or anonymized material where possible.

Initial inquiries do not authorize BoxVia to receive restricted files, credentials, private logs or production access.

Before restricted material is shared, the parties must agree in writing on the purpose, permitted materials, recipients, transfer method, allowed operations and applicable confidentiality terms.

An NDA alone does not authorize access, external transfers, AI-tool use, publication or other processing operations.

Evidence, reports and disclosure

Reports distinguish reviewed evidence, supported findings and assessment limitations.

Client evidence and reports are not published, used as public examples or distributed to additional recipients merely because a review was commissioned.

External sharing, quotation, case studies or identifying client references require a separate review of rights, confidentiality restrictions, permitted recipients and the exact proposed disclosure.

Removing names alone may not prevent identification through context or embedded metadata.

Permission to disclose selected material does not authorize changes to independent findings or publication of the complete report.

Tailored assessment arrangements

Before work starts, the parties agree in writing on the object and version, question or scenario, method, evidence, deliverable, permitted recipients, price and timing.

Restricted data, external services, AI tools and test environments require separate authorization for the relevant operations.

If necessary legal, technical or access conditions are not met, the affected operation does not proceed.
