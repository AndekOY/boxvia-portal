Check a Claim: request and payment

The Check a Claim form accepts one precise claim, a public primary-source URL and an email address. The stated price is $20 USD. After form submission, the confirmation page opens a secure PayPal checkout for the corresponding request. Submission does not constitute payment; review begins only after server-side payment confirmation. The structured English-language PDF report, normally around two pages, is delivered to the customer's submitted email address within 24 hours after payment confirmation.

If a request cannot be fulfilled within the defined scope, BoxVia will contact the requester to discuss a narrower scope or a refund, as applicable. The assessment may conclude Supported, Not supported or Unestablished within its stated boundary.

Data submitted through the form

For Check a Claim, the submitted email address, exact claim, source URL, optional version/date and required confirmations are processed by the BoxVia Cloudflare Worker and stored in its Cloudflare D1 database. This information is used to review the request, correspond with the requester, associate the PayPal payment with the request and deliver the result. Please submit only public, non-sensitive material. Do not provide passwords, confidential records or sensitive personal data.

Request processing uses Cloudflare infrastructure. PayPal handles payments under its own terms and privacy notices. After a confirmed payment, an administrative notification is sent using Resend; the customer report is delivered separately by email. Payment card details are not collected by this BoxVia form.
