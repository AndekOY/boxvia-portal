1. Submit your claim

Complete the short form with your email address, the exact claim and a public primary-source link. Include a version or date where relevant. Do not submit passwords, confidential records or sensitive personal information.

One claim only, not a request to assess an entire product or organization.
Publicly accessible official documentation or another primary source.

After successful submission, you will see the PayPal $20 payment link. We start the review only after verifying payment. If clarification is needed, we will email you.

2. Complete payment

Once your claim is submitted successfully, the confirmation page provides the secure PayPal link for $20 USD. PayPal or card checkout may be available.

We manually match your submitted request and completed payment. Use the same email where possible and keep the payment receipt. Do not pay before submitting the form.

3. Receive your result

We review one defined claim against publicly available evidence and deliver a structured PDF report in English, normally around two pages, by email.

If more information is needed, we will send specific follow-up questions to your email address.

If the request is outside the defined service scope and cannot be fulfilled, we will contact you about narrowing the request or a refund, as applicable.
