# BXC-PIZZA-TEST-VALIDATION-001
## Three Microservices — Synthetic Acceptance Test | v0.1
**2026-11-10 | INTERNAL / RELEASE HOLD**
**Scope:** Check a Claim, One Question Review, Focused AI Governance Review ONLY.
**Method:** deliberately synthetic records, not public sources or customer engagements. These tests validate logical structure and scope discipline, NOT real-world evidence quality, actual turnaround, legal compliance, or economic unit cost.
**Canonical workflow:** OBJECT → SOURCE & EVIDENCE → ASSESSMENT BOUNDARY → EVIDENCE STANDING → TRACEABLE FINDING.

## Test A — Check a Claim | USD 20 | 24 hours
**OBJECT:** "The fictional PizzaBox API v1.2 supports CSV export."
**SOURCE & EVIDENCE:** Synthetic primary-source excerpt PB-1, fictional PizzaBox API v1.2 manual, section 4: "Export formats: CSV, JSON." Source explicitly manufactured for this test. No URL, author authentication or actual product evidence.
**ASSESSMENT BOUNDARY:** textual support within supplied synthetic manual excerpt only; no software execution, no product authenticity or availability check.
**EVIDENCE STANDING:** SUPPORTED **within the synthetic test fixture**: the excerpt explicitly lists CSV. The status does not assert that any real API supports CSV.
**TRACEABLE FINDING:** PB-1 text supports the proposition for the fictional documented version. Trace: claim → PB-1 §4 → result.
**Counter-test:** If source PB-1 is unavailable or cannot be authenticated under the live product's source policy, status must change to UNESTABLISHED rather than retaining SUPPORTED.
**Quality assessment:** PASS for bounded report structure and traceability; NOT TESTED for external-source authenticity, customer readability, real production workflow or 24h delivery.
**Commercial assessment:** one source/one claim bounds effort, but no observed execution minutes or cost ledger exists; margin cannot be asserted.

## Test B — One Question Review | USD 50 | 2 days
**OBJECT:** "Does a proposed fictional vendor switch prove a 30% reduction in annual operating cost?"
**SOURCE & EVIDENCE:** Synthetic customer scenario BQ-1: baseline annual operating cost = 10,000 units; proposed vendor cost = 7,000 units; migration cost = 1,500 units in year 1; other costs assumed unchanged. All values fictional.
**ASSESSMENT BOUNDARY:** arithmetic of the supplied scenario only; no validation of actual vendor quotations, service levels, business risks, ongoing hidden costs or real realized savings.
**EVIDENCE STANDING:** NOT SUPPORTED for the claim of **30% first-year savings**. Under the fixture: year-1 cost = 7,000 + 1,500 = 8,500 units; reduction = (10,000 - 8,500)/10,000 = 15%. Recurring cost difference before other costs = 30%, but this is not first-year net savings.
**TRACEABLE FINDING:** BQ-1 assumptions imply 15% first-year cost reduction, not 30%. Actual business savings remain UNESTABLISHED without verified prices and costs. One primary status applies to the precise tested proposition; separate uncertainty is noted as a limitation, not a second status.
**Quality assessment:** PASS for independent bounded answer, clear arithmetic and distinction between modeled vs actual outcomes; NOT TESTED for real-world source review, time/cost or legal suitability of broader business questions.
**Commercial assessment:** risk of unbounded questions remains; strict admission taxonomy and effort cap required before Track A.

## Test C — Focused AI Governance Review | USD 50 | 2 days
**OBJECT:** "A fictional organization has demonstrated that its AI model monitoring control operates continuously."
**SOURCE & EVIDENCE:** Synthetic GOV-1 Policy v2 §7: "Model monitoring is performed weekly." Synthetic GOV-2 Roles v1 §3: "Model Risk Lead owns monitoring." No execution logs, tickets, alerts, signed attestations or independent operational observations are supplied. All documents fictional.
**ASSESSMENT BOUNDARY:** review of two synthetic documentary excerpts; no access to production systems and no control effectiveness testing.
**EVIDENCE STANDING:** UNESTABLISHED for **operational performance**. GOV-1 and GOV-2 support documented intent and assigned ownership, but cannot establish that monitoring actually occurs. "Weekly" also does not independently establish continuous operation.
**TRACEABLE FINDING:** GOV-1 and GOV-2 evidence documented design and ownership; implementation and operating effectiveness are not established. Trace: proposition → GOV-1/GOV-2 → evidence gap → result.
**Quality assessment:** PASS for distinguishing design evidence from operating evidence; NOT TESTED for actual governance document provenance, GDPR, practical 2-day delivery or 50 USD unit economics.
**Commercial assessment:** only bounded public/authorized document review can be standardized; internal policies/logs require Track B or refusal.

## Findings and corrections
F01 — Status is **relative to the exact assessed proposition**. A document can support policy existence while operational effectiveness is UNESTABLISHED. Do not conflate these.
F02 — One Question Review must distinguish arithmetic/model results from verified real-world savings and exclude unbounded or regulated advice.
F03 — Check a Claim must define a response to inaccessible/unverifiable sources and distinguish documentary support from truth.
F04 — For Focused AI Governance Review, distinguish policy existence, ownership assignment, implementation and effectiveness.
F05 — A report is an independently paid result even if NOT SUPPORTED or UNESTABLISHED, subject to actual performance and mandatory customer remedies.
F06 — Do not treat the synthetic PASS as legal, operational, commercial or methodological certification.
F07 — Product cards must define source/document caps, time budget, language, delivery format, exact clock triggers and complaint/refund workflow before public offer approval.
F08 — Pizza-Test source fixtures must be unmistakably labeled SYNTHETIC and must never be published as factual customer case studies.
