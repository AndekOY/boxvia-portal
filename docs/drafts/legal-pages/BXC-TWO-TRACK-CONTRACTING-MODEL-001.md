# BXC-TWO-TRACK-CONTRACTING-MODEL-001
## BoxVia Two-Track Contracting Model | v0.1 | INTERNAL DRAFT / RELEASE HOLD
Date: 2026-11-09
Status: OWNER PROPOSAL ACCEPTED FOR DRAFTING; legal/tax approval and publication pending.

## 1. Policy decision
BoxVia separates contracting into:
**Track A — Public Offer + Standard Order** for low-complexity, standardized, fixed-price services whose scope, inputs, price, timing and deliverables are predefined and that do not require enhanced confidentiality.
**Track B — Individual Agreement + Statement of Work** for material, bespoke, complex, regulated, higher-risk or confidential engagements, including those requiring nonstandard IP, security, privacy or disclosure terms.

Price is an indicator, not the sole classifier. A low-price request with sensitive data, regulated advice, third-party secrets, significant legal risk or custom confidentiality is escalated to Track B or declined. No monetary threshold is yet approved.

## 2. Track A: contractual structure
- Public General Terms / Public Offer with version/date and provider identity.
- Product-specific Service Schedule: scope, exclusions, evidence inputs, output, language, turnaround, delivery/access channel, price and VAT treatment.
- Order summary before payment; customer category and applicable statutory notices.
- Clear acceptance mechanism: offer/order acceptance and when contract is formed must be defined against the actual checkout flow, not presumed from PayPal success.
- Customer receives confirmation and applicable terms on a durable medium.
- Consumer withdrawal, express request for early performance and any statutory acknowledgment must be handled only as legally appropriate to the classified service/digital content; no blanket waiver.
- Privacy notice and data handling; public forms reject confidential/sensitive inputs.
- Complaints, statutory remedies, refunds and disputes are not overridden by a no-refund clause.

## 3. Track B: contractual structure
- Individual Agreement / Master Services Agreement as applicable.
- Statement of Work (SoW): defined assessment object, boundaries, methodology, evidence, deliverables, milestones, price, change control and acceptance.
- NDA/confidentiality and publication/quotation permissions.
- Data processing arrangement where actual controller/processor roles require it; NDA alone is not a GDPR DPA.
- IP/licensing: client rights to deliverables vs ANDEKS/BCAF preexisting methods and frameworks.
- Liability allocation, third-party claims, permitted reliance, no implied certification/legal opinion, dispute resolution, applicable law and jurisdiction consistent with mandatory law.
- Explicit authority to provide inputs, handling of regulated data, access controls and retention.
- Signature/acceptance method and contract archive.

## 4. Escalation triggers A -> B
1. Confidential or nonpublic client data, trade secrets, credentials, sensitive personal data.
2. Custom scope or material deviations from the published service schedule.
3. Regulated-sector or legal/tax decision reliance.
4. Multiple organizations, third-party rights or disputed authority to disclose.
5. Custom IP assignment, publication restrictions, special security or hosting.
6. Nonstandard liability, payment terms, delivery obligations or audit rights.
7. Unclear consumer status, territorial restrictions or tax treatment needing case review.
On trigger: stop standard checkout and request an individual scoping process; do not accept sensitive material in the public intake.

## 5. Product mapping — provisional
- Check a Claim ($20): Track A by default, subject to standard-input gate.
- Fixed-price microservices ($50): Track A only if predefined and low-risk.
- Technical Pilot: Track A for a strictly standardized synthetic/public-input package; Track B if bespoke, confidential or material reliance.
- Complex assessment / bespoke research / confidential BCAF engagements: Track B.

## 6. Legal decision gates
L01 verify provider registry and public contact.
L02 determine exact offer/acceptance/contract formation event and durable-medium confirmation.
L03 Polish/EU consumer withdrawal, early commencement and digital-service classification reviewed by qualified counsel.
L04 VAT/tax and cross-border classification reviewed.
L05 GDPR purposes/bases, provider roles and retention approved.
L06 precise escalation and refusal workflow implemented.
L07 bilingual EN/RU technical parity and versioned evidence.
L08 explicit owner approval before publication or production implementation.

## 7. Documents to draft next
A: Public Offer/General Terms; product schedules; order summary; consumer notices/withdrawal and complaint instructions; Privacy Notice.
B: Individual Agreement; SoW; NDA; DPA only when roles require; change order; acceptance/delivery certificate or equivalent record.

## 8. Restrictions
No automatic conclusion that PayPal payment alone forms contract or removes withdrawal rights.
No universal no-refund promise.
No implied client transfer of ANDEKS/BCAF IP.
No publication of client cases without lawful authorization.
No production, site or checkout changes authorized.

## RU — Резюме для операционного применения
Поток A: стандартная публичная оферта + индивидуализированный заказ для недорогих типовых услуг без специальных условий конфиденциальности.
Поток B: индивидуальный договор + техническое задание/SoW для сложных, конфиденциальных и нестандартных работ.
Критерий — не только цена, но и содержание данных, риск, индивидуальность задания и условия использования результата.
Вопросы акцепта, потребительских прав, налогов, GDPR и сроков хранения остаются на юридическом согласовании.
