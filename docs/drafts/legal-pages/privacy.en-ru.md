### EN — source-evidenced, not yet effective
The current public `/privacy/` page identifies `andrzej.ekhmenin@gmail.com` as privacy contact; **this is a published address, not independent confirmation that it is the approved or operational statutory mailbox**. The current code uses Cloudflare Workers and D1 `DB` (database `boxvia-claims`). Check a Claim intake stores email, exact claim, source URL and optional source version/date (the latter in a notes field), alongside request/payment status. A hashed access capability is stored in D1; the browser holds the original access capability and PayPal order reference in sessionStorage. The Check a Claim Turnstile verification request explicitly forwards the request IP address to Cloudflare as `remoteip`. Separate service request forms send customer email and service-specific text to the configured operator email through Resend. After a confirmed payment, an administrative email with claim ID and customer email can be queued and sent via Resend, depending on runtime and Cron configuration. Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon scripts appear on inspected HTML pages. The inspected notification handler does **not** demonstrate that the customer receives the legally required durable contract confirmation. These facts describe code paths; actual production flags, Cron, provider configuration, logs, retention and browser tracking remain unverified.

**Unresolved GDPR Art. 13 fields:** verified controller/contact/registered address; category-by-category legal bases; provider roles and contracts; recipients/subprocessors; international transfer mechanisms; retention periods or specific criteria; automated decision-making, if any; cookie/terminal-storage inventory; verified rights-request channel. ## v0.4 — D1 schema verified from production metadata export, 2026-11-09
### EN — proposed publication wording, subject to legal/retention verification
When you submit a Check a Claim request, BoxVia records your email address, the claim text, creation date, and request and payment statuses. Depending on your submission and processing, the record may also include a source URL, report URL and operational notes. To manage request access, we store a token hash linked to the request. To process and reconcile payments, we store PayPal order identifiers, amounts, currency, payment status and, where applicable, a capture identifier. To manage service emails, we store notification event type, delivery status, retry count, timestamps and possible delivery errors. The underlying payment card details are not fields of these four application tables; payments are handled through PayPal. Do not infer from this that no other provider processes card or device data.

**Retention is not yet verified:** No duration should be asserted from this schema alone. The applicable retention schedule, deletion process, D1 backup/Time Travel retention and processors must be confirmed before publication.

### RU — технический перевод
При подаче заявки Check a Claim BoxVia сохраняет адрес электронной почты, текст утверждения, дату создания, статусы заявки и оплаты. В зависимости от заявки и ее обработки запись также может содержать URL источника, URL отчета и служебные заметки. Для управления доступом к заявке хранится связанный с ней хеш токена.
