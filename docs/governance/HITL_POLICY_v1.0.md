Status: APPROVED - owner decision 2026-11-15
Owner: Andrey Ekhmenin
Scope: BoxVia services, public communications, assessments and report production
Canonical repository: AndekOY/boxvia-portal

## Positioning
**BoxVia - Human-Accountable Independent Assessment.**
**AI-assisted. Human-assessed. Evidence-based.**

## Policy
1. AI may assist research, extraction, comparison, analysis and drafting within the authorized scope.
2. A responsible human defines the assessment question, object/version, scope, evidence boundary and release criteria.
3. Material evidence and citations are checked by the responsible human before a finding is released.
4. AI-generated findings are proposals, not final independent judgments.
5. The responsible human evaluates the available evidence, determines the final finding, records limitations and authorizes release.
6. Reports identify the responsible assessor and disclose material AI assistance where applicable, without claiming all text was manually authored.
7. An assessment cannot be called human-assessed or human-reviewed if it was issued without substantive human verification and release authorization.
8. Human accountability does not imply certification, guaranteed correctness, regulatory approval or unlimited liability.
9. Client confidentiality, data access and AI-tool use remain subject to separate permissions and applicable law. HITL is not an exception to BCAF safeguards.
10. Any workflow that cannot demonstrate the controls above must be labeled as automated, draft or unverified, not as a human-assessed independent assessment.

## Authority boundary
This is an approved BoxVia operating policy. It does not amend ANDEKS normative releases, approve draft BCAF documents, or override contracts, law or specific assessment protocols.

## Change control
Changes to this policy require explicit owner approval, a new version and a dated change record.
