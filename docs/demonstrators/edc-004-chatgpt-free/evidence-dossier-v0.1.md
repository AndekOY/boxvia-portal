## 3. Assessment questions

- Q1 — Permission: do applicable terms permit commercial use of text output for ChatGPT Free?
- Q2 — Ownership: what contractual rights, if any, does OpenAI assign or recognize for the user, and subject to which qualifications?
- Q3 — Non-infringement: does that permission imply an OpenAI warranty that output is unique, copyrightable or does not infringe third-party rights?

Q3 is a boundary test, not a claim that OpenAI necessarily makes or disclaims a particular warranty; record the precise terms before answering.

## 4. Assessment boundary

**Included:** individual adult ChatGPT Free account, ordinary text output, public contractual documents, EU/EEA applicability, commercial use as defined by the documents.

**Excluded:** API, Enterprise/Business contracts, custom negotiated terms, third-party GPT/app licenses, images/audio/video, input ownership disputes, copyright registration, national legal opinions, infringement adjudication, factual accuracy and professional reliance.

**Time boundary:** one dated snapshot of applicable documents at the time of evidence collection. Changes to terms require a new assessment.
