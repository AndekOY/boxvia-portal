**Critical methodological correction for EDC-004:** the approved template is a **single-claim, single-primary-source** check. Therefore the report must identify one controlling primary source and restrict the formal finding to what it establishes. Other OpenAI documents may be background/context in the research dossier but must not silently turn the report into a multi-source assessment. For EDC-004 choose the official OpenAI Help Center FAQ `https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6783457` as the direct primary source for Free-plan commercial use, or change the claim and use the European Terms as sole primary source.
