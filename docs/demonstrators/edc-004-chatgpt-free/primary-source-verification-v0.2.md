## Evidence and interpretation

**E1 / Commercial use — S3, FAQ “Can I use output from ChatGPT for commercial uses?”**

The FAQ states that, subject to applicable policies and terms, users may reprint, sell and merchandise ChatGPT output regardless of whether it was generated on a free or paid plan. This is explicit first-party evidence for the *qualified* commercial-use claim.

**E2 / Rights as between user and OpenAI — S1, “Ownership of content”**

The Europe Terms state that, as between user and OpenAI and to the extent permitted by law, the user owns Output; OpenAI assigns any rights it has in Output. This contractual allocation does not establish that every output is eligible for statutory copyright or that no third-party rights apply.

**E3 / Non-exclusivity and third parties — S1, “Similarity of content”; “Third party Services”; “Your content”**

OpenAI warns that output may not be unique and similar output may be given to others; the assignment does not cover other users' output or Third Party Output. The user is responsible for content and compliance.

**E4 / Review obligations — S1, “Accuracy”; “Using our Services”**

Users must assess output for accuracy and appropriateness, comply with law and policies, and avoid infringing others' rights.

**E5 / Boundary exclusions — S2, §§1, 3, 7, 8**

Separate indemnification provisions for API and certain business offerings must not be projected onto ChatGPT Free. ChatGPT Voice Output is expressly limited to non-commercial use, and third-party Apps can introduce third-party content. This demonstrator therefore covers ordinary **text** output only, without an App-specific third-party content claim.

## Controlled assessment

**Claim C1 (EN):** An individual using ChatGPT Free in the EEA may commercially use ordinary text output generated through the service, subject to applicable OpenAI terms and laws.

**Утверждение C1 (RU):** Физическое лицо, использующее ChatGPT Free в ЕЭЗ, может применять обычные текстовые результаты сервиса в коммерческих целях при соблюдении применимых условий OpenAI и законодательства.

**Finding C1: SUPPORTED — QUALIFIED.** Direct evidence S3 plus contractual context S1 supports this precisely bounded proposition.

**Subclaim C2:** The user receives, as between user and OpenAI and insofar as legally permitted, contractual rights in Output.

**Finding C2: SUPPORTED — QUALIFIED** by S1. This does **not** determine copyrightability, exclusivity, registration or ownership against third parties.

**Subclaim C3:** OpenAI guarantees all text output is unique and free of third-party IP claims.

**Finding C3: NOT SUPPORTED** by the inspected documents: S1 expressly warns of similar outputs and third-party exclusions; no universal guarantee is established. This is a documentary finding, **not** a judicial determination of any particular infringement.
