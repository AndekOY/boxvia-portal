## 1. Applicable law
Consumers and, where legally entitled, natural-person sole traders receive the protections provided by applicable EU and Polish law, including the Polish Consumer Rights Act (ustawa o prawach konsumenta).
Mandatory rights prevail over any inconsistent contract wording.
The customer's actual legal status is determined by law and the circumstances, not solely by a checkbox.

## 2. Before payment
Before the customer is bound, BoxVia provides the information required by applicable law, including provider identity and contact, service characteristics and scope, final payable price and applicable taxes, payment and delivery arrangements, complaint channels, withdrawal information and the model withdrawal form.
The checkout must clearly identify the obligation to pay and preserve the versions of the information shown to the customer.

## 3. Withdrawal and early performance
For a qualifying distance contract for services, the withdrawal period is generally 14 days from contract conclusion, subject to statutory exceptions and extensions.
The separate Withdrawal Notice explains how to exercise the right and supplies the model form.
Performance during the withdrawal period requires any express request and acknowledgements prescribed by applicable law; the relevant controls must be separate and initially unchecked.
Any proportionate payment following withdrawal, and any loss of withdrawal rights after full performance, arise only where the applicable statutory conditions are met.
Payment or acceptance of these Terms alone does not constitute an early-performance request.

## 4. Performance and remedies
The 24-hour execution period begins only after the lawful and operational start conditions in the Order are met.
Mandatory rules on conformity, complaints, remedies, refunds and liability apply, including any protections relevant to the actual legal classification of the supplied service or digital deliverable.
No waiver of statutory remedies, mandatory jurisdiction rights or consumer protection is imposed.

## 5. Confirmation
BoxVia provides the required confirmation of the concluded distance contract on a durable medium, including the accepted Order and required information, within the applicable statutory timeframe and before performance begins where required.
Evidence of disclosure, affirmative actions, payment verification and confirmation dispatch is retained.

Release restriction: final Polish/EU consumer-law review, checkout implementation and owner approval required.
