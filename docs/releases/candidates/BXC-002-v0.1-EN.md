## 1. Application
This Schedule applies to consumers and, where mandatory law extends relevant protections, natural-person sole traders entering a transaction not professional in character. The actual circumstances determine coverage; a customer declaration is not the sole test and must not be improperly required as a condition of contracting. Mandatory law prevails.

## 2. Information before a binding order
Before purchase, BoxVia supplies the verified seller identity and contact, service characteristics, exact scope, final payable price and legally required tax information, payment and delivery arrangements, complaint procedure, applicable withdrawal notice and statutory model form, and other mandatory consumer information. The final order action must unambiguously state the obligation to pay. The displayed versions and evidence of disclosure are retained.

## 3. Withdrawal
For a typical Polish distance contract for services, the statutory withdrawal period is generally 14 days from conclusion, subject to applicable mandatory law and exceptions. Where mandatory information has not been given, the statutory consequences apply. The customer may exercise the right by an unequivocal statement; the model form is available but is not compulsory for exercising the right. The exact Withdrawal Notice accompanies the Order.

## 4. Early performance and 24-hour service
A customer may separately and expressly request performance during the withdrawal period, using an optional, initially unchecked control. BoxVia separately communicates and records any legally required information and acknowledgment about proportional payment and possible loss of withdrawal rights after full performance. Acceptance of Terms or payment alone is not an early-performance request. If valid early commencement is not requested or cannot lawfully occur, the 24-hour performance clock does not start; the order must state the actual lawful start rule. No blanket waiver or no-refund rule is used.

## 5. Performance, conformity and remedies
Mandatory consumer remedies are not excluded by acceptance of an analytical conclusion. An evidence-supported unfavorable result is not by itself a defect where the contracted process and deliverable were provided correctly. The applicable statutory regime for a service versus digital content or digital service must be determined from the actual transaction and delivery.

## 6. Complaints, refunds and disputes
Complaints may be sent to andrzej.ekhmenin@gmail.com. BoxVia records receipt and responds within the applicable statutory period. Refunds, proportionate payment, price reduction, conformity remedies and dispute resolution follow mandatory law. No forced arbitration or waiver of consumer jurisdiction protections applies. Any mandatory ADR information must be provided.

## 7. Checkout and durable confirmation
The Order displays the exact Terms and Schedule versions, scope, price, mandatory notices, withdrawal form and separate early-performance controls. BoxVia preserves evidence of each affirmative action and provides contract confirmation on a durable medium as required by applicable law, including before commencement where required. No controls are preselected.
