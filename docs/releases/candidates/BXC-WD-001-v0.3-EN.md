You may use the model form below, but you do not have to.
To meet the deadline, it is sufficient to send your statement before the withdrawal period expires.
Mandatory statutory exceptions and any statutory extension due to missing information apply under the Polish Consumer Rights Act (ustawa o prawach konsumenta).

## Effects of withdrawal
If you withdraw validly, we will reimburse all payments received for this contract without undue delay and no later than 14 days after we are informed of your decision to withdraw, subject to applicable mandatory law.
We will reimburse using the same payment method used for the original transaction unless you expressly agree otherwise; you will not incur any fee as a result of the reimbursement.
If you expressly requested that the service begin during the withdrawal period, you may be required to pay an amount proportionate to the service supplied up to the time you informed us of withdrawal, but only where the applicable statutory conditions for such payment are satisfied.
For a service contract, the withdrawal right may cease after full performance only where the legally required prior express request/consent and informed acknowledgment have been obtained.
Accepting general terms or making payment does not by itself waive your right.
Your applicable statutory rights under EU and Polish law remain unaffected.
