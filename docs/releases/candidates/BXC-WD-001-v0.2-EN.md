## Right of withdrawal
Where the statutory right applies to a distance contract for services, you generally have 14 days from conclusion of the contract to withdraw without giving a reason, subject to the applicable rules and exceptions under EU and Polish consumer law, including the Polish Consumer Rights Act (ustawa o prawach konsumenta).
To exercise your right, notify BoxVia of your decision by an unequivocal statement sent before the applicable deadline to andrzej.ekhmenin@gmail.com or to the postal address below.
You may use the model form, but its use is not mandatory.
Where required withdrawal information was not provided, statutory extensions and consequences apply.

## Effects of withdrawal
If you validly withdraw, BoxVia will reimburse the payments subject to the applicable statutory rules, generally within 14 days after receiving your withdrawal notice, using the original payment method unless another method is expressly agreed without additional cost.
If you expressly requested early performance and then withdraw, proportionate payment for work actually performed may be payable only where the law permits and all statutory conditions are satisfied.
The right of withdrawal is lost following full performance only if the relevant statutory requirements have been met; accepting general Terms or making payment does not automatically waive it.
The precise legal treatment depends on whether the transaction is classified as a service, digital content or a digital service.
