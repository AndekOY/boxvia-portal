ERA - Evidence Readiness Assessment

ERA examines whether the available evidence is enough to support a specific claim. It records what can be checked, what can be repeated and what is still missing.

Read the ERA overview

What ERA examines

  • The exact claim and the scope of the review.
  • The evidence available for inspection.
  • The checks another reviewer can repeat.
  • Missing records or verification steps.
  • The conclusion that the examined evidence supports.

A practical example

A system claims that its records can be independently verified. ERA asks whether a reviewer has the records, the verification rules and the inputs needed to repeat the check.

If key material is missing, the result may remain UNESTABLISHED within the review's scope. This identifies a limit in the available evidence.

How the systems connect

ERA is built on ARE, the ANDEKS Runtime Engine. The ANDEKS™ Normative System provides a separate framework and methodology for independent normative assessment.
