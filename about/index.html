About BoxVia

BoxVia is an independent business operator registered in Poland and providing analytical, research and technology-related services internationally.

Our activities combine professional services, independent assessment, applied research and technology development, with a focus on AI governance, evidence assessment, decision systems, regulatory architecture and technology assurance.

Professional services

BoxVia provides focused analytical services covering specific questions, pre-decision reviews, AI governance and compliance, regulatory architecture and evidence-based gap assessments.

Each engagement is defined by its scope, available evidence, expected deliverable and explicit limitations.
