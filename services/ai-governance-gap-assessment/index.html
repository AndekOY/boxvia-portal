Start request

When this fits

You have a defined AI system or process and an agreed set of requirements to examine.

You need to understand which controls are supported by evidence and which gaps matter.

What to send

Start with the system boundary, intended use, proposed requirements and an outline of available policies, control records and technical evidence.

The assessment criteria and exclusions are agreed before substantive review.

What you receive

A written map connecting the selected requirements to controls, evidence and identified gaps.

The report distinguishes demonstrated gaps from missing or conflicting evidence.

Findings include their basis, significance, limitations and agreed priorities for further work.

Remediation implementation is separately scoped.

Choose the right scope

For one narrow governance question, use the Focused AI Governance Review.

If the project itself is not yet defined, consider FAAR Stage 1.

The starting price covers a small agreed assessment, rather than a full organization-wide compliance program.

This assessment provides no legal opinion, certification, audit assurance or guarantee of compliance.

A missing record does not automatically establish that a control is absent.
