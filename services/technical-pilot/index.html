How independent verification works

The pilot does not simply reproduce the enforcement implementation or assume its output is correct.

The assessment does not assume that the system owner's logs, policy decisions or test results are complete or correct.

We distinguish three elements:

  1. Authorized boundary: the applicable policy, scope, delegation and state, as documented by the system owner.
  2. Observed execution: the proposed action, policy decision, tool invocation, outcome and relevant state transitions.
  3. Independent evidence review: whether available records and agreed tests support a bounded finding about the observed behavior.

The system owner remains responsible for defining policy, granting authority and operating enforcement.

BoxVia does not become the enterprise decision authority or an inline execution gate.

Examples from completed research

WEBAGENT.AI - What does observed AI behavior establish?

A completed comparative research pilot examined a public AI support agent against frozen public statements and observed answers.

Selected factual answers were supported, while broader claims such as 24/7 availability and the origin of answers were not established from the limited observations.

The independent ANDEKS result was PARTIALLY ESTABLISHED / MATERIAL LIMITATIONS. The later comparative record was mutually reviewed without changing either frozen result.

OMNIX - Does a successful verification status mean full verification?

In a bounded independent reproduction of an early public verifier, a synthetic unsigned receipt could return fully_verified=true when the implemented hash, authority-budget, status and expiry checks passed.

This establishes the behavior of that specific verification logic, not cryptographic authenticity of a real receipt or end-to-end verification of the wider system.

R1–R6 were reproduced on synthetic inputs; later verification paths were not established end-to-end.

Historical research illustrations only, not paid customer engagements, certification results or evidence of current production behavior.

What you receive

One concise written report covering the assessment question, system version and scope, method or evidence reviewed, observed findings, evidence references, gaps, conclusion and limitations.

Findings may establish a bounded observation, identify a demonstrated deviation or remain UNESTABLISHED when evidence is insufficient.

A reproduced failure does not automatically establish its root cause. A successful tool response does not by itself establish valid authority or effective enforcement.
