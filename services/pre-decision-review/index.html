Independent Pre-Decision Review

Check the evidence behind one specific decision before you commit.

200 USD - 3 days

Delivery time starts when BoxVia receives the agreed inputs.

Start request

What to send

Send the proposed decision, its context, any options under consideration and the available evidence.

For example, ask what the evidence supports before accepting a defined supplier proposal.

What you receive

You receive a written review of the decision and its scope, the evidence for key assumptions and any gaps that matter before you commit.

If the project scope itself is undefined, a separately agreed FAAR Stage 1 may be more appropriate.

Stage 1 is not included in the 200 USD decision review.

Findings apply to the agreed question and evidence.

The review provides no legal opinion, audit assurance or certification.
