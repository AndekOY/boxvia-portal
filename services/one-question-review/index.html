METHODOLOGY IN PRACTICE

Sample report: is the promised 30% saving supported?

A fictional client asks whether a vendor's automation proposal substantiates a 30% annual reduction in operating costs, taking first-year implementation and support charges into account.

SUPPORTED

The vendor's narrow comparison of USD 120,000 and USD 84,000 produces a 30% arithmetic difference.

NOT SUPPORTED

The full first-year model yields an 8.3% net saving, not 30%.

UNESTABLISHED

Realized savings remain unverified without pilot results and reconciled operating records.

This illustrative example uses fictional evidence and does not represent a client engagement.
