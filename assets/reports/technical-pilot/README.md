# Technical pilot report samples

Prepared for BoxVia on 5 November 2026.

Each English PDF has two pages and is an anonymized, shortened adaptation of a real historical record.

These samples illustrate reporting practice rather than establish the current condition of a named system.

| Sample | Scope | Key distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 - Support agent | Black-box behavior review | Correct local answers do not establish every public promise. |
| 02 - Evidence traceability | Documentary evidence review | Reproducible arithmetic does not establish the exact measurement basis or data provenance. |
| 03 - Signature verification | Independent public-surface technical review | A reproduced verification failure does not establish its root cause. |

Names, product identifiers, URLs, exact observation dates, identifying quantities, hashes, key material and direct quotations have been removed or generalized where applicable.

The historical sources date from August and September 2026.

The original findings have not been reclassified under a later ANDEKS release.

No new execution, reproduction or formal normative assessment was performed to create these PDFs.

The samples are not authoritative replacements for the original records and do not claim client endorsement, certification, production approval or deployed ARE/ERA processing.

Sample 03 represents a closed public phase with an unresolved cause, rather than completion of an agreed private-beta engagement.
